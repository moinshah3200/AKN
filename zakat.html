Zakat Calculator

Work out the 2.5% due on your wealth by the gold or silver nisab. Everything is computed on your own device — nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Most scholars advise the silver nisab as the safer basis for giving — it sets the lower threshold, so more zakat reaches the deserving.

Enter the silver price per gram to work out your nisab.

Zakat falls due on wealth held for one full lunar year at or above the nisab — 87.48 g of gold or 612.36 g of silver at today’s market price. This calculator applies the standard 2.5% rate; for business assets, shares, pensions or any unusual case, please consult a scholar.
