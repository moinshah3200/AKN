Emergency response

Flood Relief 2025

When the floods came, AKN teams reached thirteen towns and districts — from Buner and Mingora to Shakargarh and Kasur — with ration for 1,290 families, cooked food for 10,600 people, ₨ 15,00,000 in cash aid, hand pumps, charpais and 265 blankets.
