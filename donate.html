Zakat guidance

Not sure how much zakat you owe?

Use our free calculator: enter your cash, gold, silver, business stock and receivables, choose the gold or silver nisab, and it works out the 2.5% due — entirely on your device.

For unusual cases, please consult a scholar. Your zakat reaches the deserving — and we can collect it from your home.
