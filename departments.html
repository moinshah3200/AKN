Department 01
Dawat-e-Islam

Da’wah on the Prophetic method

The Trust carries out da’wah with sincerity, on the method of the Prophet ﷺ. Through these efforts, hundreds of Qadianis have accepted Islam — among them Shams-ud-Din, foster nephew of the current Qadiani head Mirza Masroor; the former murabbis Amir Munir, Nazir Ahmad and Shahid Ahmad; the former mufti Muhammad Ahmad; and many members of the Mirza family.

Alongside this, the doubts of thousands of Muslims have been resolved.

Hundreds embraced Islam Thousands of doubts resolved
Ration Distribution

Monthly ration, 90,000+ people reached

Through the monthly ration programme, ration has reached approximately 90,000+ people to date, with priority given to new Muslims, the needy, widows and orphans. One family’s package costs ₨ 6,000 — a month of food security for a household.

90,000+ people reached ₨ 6,000 per family package

Department 03
Monthly Stipends

Cash stipends for the deserving

Widows, orphans and poor women in Chenab Nagar receive regular cash stipends from the Trust — protecting them from Qadiani exploitation and giving them a dependable, dignified income.

Widows · orphans · poor women ₨ 32,59,000 given in 2025
Employment

Work in place of boycott

New Muslims often face a social boycott. The Trust answers it with livelihoods: rickshaws for young men, sewing machines for widows, help finding private-sector jobs for educated new Muslims, and qarz-e-hasana (interest-free loans) to start businesses.

Rickshaws & sewing machines Qarz-e-hasana business loans

Department 05
Water Supply

Clean water from Thar to Punjab

Wells in interior Sindh — Tharparkar’s Mithi, Khokhrapar, Nangarpar and Islamkot — with electric pumps for the desert wells; hand pumps installed in 20 homes in Chenab Nagar; and water filtration plants in Punjab worth lakhs of rupees, serving thousands of people.

Wells in Tharparkar Hand pumps in 20 homes Filtration plants in Punjab
Quranic Schools (Makatib)

The Quran in every child’s hands

Makatib established in Lahore, Chenab Nagar, other parts of Punjab and interior Sindh, where hundreds of children have completed their Quranic education — with the protection of aqeedah woven into every lesson.

13 makatib 2,000+ children under education

Department 07
Marriage (Nikah)

New homes, built on dignity

The Trust arranges the nikah of new-Muslim daughters, protecting them from social isolation; finds rishtas for new-Muslim men and women; and stands by the couples with support in their married life.

Rishtas for new Muslims ₨ 5,50,000 on nikah in 2025
Darul Kafalah

A roof for new Muslims in Lahore

A two-storey residential building beside the central office at Rajgarh, Lahore, built at approximately ₨ 1 crore, provides free residence for new Muslims. 8 more flats are planned.

Free residence ≈ ₨ 1 crore building 8 more flats planned

Department 09
Modern & Technical Education

From school fees to freelancing

The Trust bears school, college and university expenses for new-Muslim students — fees, uniform and stationery. A school for approximately 300 children in Ahmad Nagar (District Chiniot) is underway, and a college is planned at the Khatm-e-Nabuwat office, Rajgarh.

Technical training for new Muslims and the poor covers electrician and plumber trades, IT courses, digital marketing and freelancing.

300-child school underway IT · digital marketing · trades
Your Home Is Your Paradise

Homes with ownership rights

The Trust builds homes — with ownership rights — for new Muslims and widows; arranges rented homes for new Muslims in emergencies; and carries monthly rent support worth lakhs of rupees.

Ownership rights Emergency rented homes Rent support worth lakhs

Department 11
Khatm-e-Nabuwat Courses

Training defenders of the faith

Three-day short courses, 15-day and monthly courses run across Pakistan’s cities. A special two-month management course covers project management, personal development, journalism, Quranic Arabic, the art of speech (khitabat), munazara, mubahasa and mukalama.

3-day · 15-day · monthly Two-month management course
Broadcasting

The message, in print and online

Literature and books for the protection of Khatm-e-Nabuwat and awareness of the Qadiani fitnah; the monthly magazine «انوار ختم نبوت» (Anwar Khatm-e-Nabuwat); the AKN63Channel web channel; and an active presence on Facebook, YouTube and other social platforms.

Monthly magazine AKN63Channel Books & literature
