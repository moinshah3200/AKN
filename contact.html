Contact us

Call or WhatsApp for zakat and donations, welfare requests, da’wah matters or the Rafiq Medical Center. There is no contact form on this site — every reply comes personally, by phone or WhatsApp.

Zakat, sadaqat and atiyat are received at all banks and at the Medical Centre offices — and our team can collect from your home. No bank account numbers are published here; please confirm details by phone or WhatsApp.

In person

Visit or write

The Trust’s markaz is in Chenab Nagar (Mohalla Nusratabad), District Chiniot, Punjab — the home of its da’wah work and of the Rafiq Medical Center now under construction. You are welcome to visit, meet the team and see the work first-hand.

In Lahore, the central office at 55-Ghani Street, Rajgarh sits beside Darul Kafalah, the Trust’s two-storey residence offering free housing to new Muslims. Letters may be addressed to either office.

  • Markaz — Chenab Nagar (Mohalla Nusratabad), District Chiniot, Punjab
  • Central office & Darul Kafalah — 55-Ghani Street, Rajgarh, Lahore
