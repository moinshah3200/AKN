## Departments (from the profile, in profile order)

1. **Dawat-e-Islam** — da'wah on the Prophetic method with sincerity; hundreds of Qadianis have accepted
   Islam through the Trust's efforts, among them Shams-ud-Din (foster nephew of the current Qadiani head
   Mirza Masroor), former murabbis Amir Munir, Nazir Ahmad, Shahid Ahmad, former mufti Muhammad Ahmad,
   and many members of the Mirza family; thousands of Muslims' doubts resolved.
2. **Ration Distribution** — a monthly ration programme that has reached approximately **90,000+ people** to date (per profile p5 — cumulative reach, not a per-month count); priority to new Muslims,
   the needy, widows and orphans; **one family's package costs ₨ 6,000**.
3. **Monthly Stipends** — cash stipends for deserving people: widows, orphans, poor women in Chenab Nagar,
   protecting them from Qadiani exploitation.
4. **Employment** — counteracting the social boycott of new Muslims: rickshaws for young men,
   sewing machines for widows, help finding private-sector jobs for educated new Muslims,
   qarz-e-hasana (interest-free loans) to start businesses.
5. **Water Supply** — wells in interior Sindh (Tharparkar: Mithi, Khokhrapar, Nangarpar, Islamkot),
   electric pumps for desert wells, hand pumps in **20 homes in Chenab Nagar**, water filtration plants
   in Punjab worth lakhs of rupees serving thousands.
6. **Quranic Schools (Makatib)** — established in Lahore, Chenab Nagar, other parts of Punjab and interior
   Sindh; hundreds of children have completed Quranic education; aqeedah protection included.
7. **Marriage (Nikah)** — arranging nikah of new-Muslim daughters, protecting them from social isolation;
   rishtas for new-Muslim men and women; married life support.
8. **Darul Kafalah** — two-storey residential building beside the central office at **Rajgarh, Lahore**,
   built at approximately **₨ 1 crore**; free residence for new Muslims; **8 more flats** planned.
9. **Modern & Technical Education** — school/college/university expenses for new-Muslim students
   (fees, uniform, stationery); a school for approximately **300 children** in **Ahmad Nagar (District
   Chiniot)** underway; technical training for new Muslims and the poor (electrician, plumber, IT courses,
   digital marketing, freelancing); a college planned at the Khatm-e-Nabuwat office Rajgarh.
10. **Your Home Is Your Paradise** — building homes with ownership rights for new Muslims and widows;
    rented homes for new Muslims in emergencies; monthly rent support worth lakhs of rupees.
11. **Khatm-e-Nabuwat Courses** — 3-day short courses, 15-day and monthly courses across Pakistan's cities;
    a special two-month management course covering project management, personal development, journalism,
    Quranic Arabic, art of speech (khitabat), munazara, mubahasa and mukalama.
12. **Broadcasting** — literature and books for the protection of Khatm-e-Nabuwat and awareness of the
    Qadiani fitnah; monthly magazine **«انوار ختم نبوت»**; **AKN63Channel** web channel; Facebook, YouTube
    and other social platforms.
13. **Report of Acceptance of Islam** — hundreds of Qadianis have accepted Islam, including **4 former
    murabbis and one former mufti** (p8 prints the name 'Shahid' with the mufti; NOTE: the profile conflicts internally — p5 lists Shahid Ahmad as a former murabbi and Muhammad Ahmad as the former mufti; site copy therefore names neither); **350+** Qadianis through the Trust's preachers; in **2025**,
    **50 people** repented from Qadianiyat and Christianity and accepted Islam.
