Rafiq Medical Center

رفیق میڈیکل سینٹر — a welfare project by AKN Trust in Chenab Nagar: a charitable hospital with OPD, operation theatre, gynae centre, laboratory, colour-doppler ultrasound, medical store and ambulance service.

Chenab Nagar · under construction
Why this hospital

Healthcare where it is needed most

The Rafiq Medical Center will bring proper medical care to the people of Chenab Nagar and its surroundings, as sadaqah jariyah in the path of Allah.

The centre is planned with these departments:

  • Outdoor Clinic (OPD)
  • Operation Theatre
  • Gynae Centre
  • Laboratory
  • Ultrasound — Colour Doppler
  • Medical Store
  • Ambulance service

Construction progress

From foundations to a rising structure

Construction began in 2025. Alhamdulillah, the first roof slab (lenter) is already complete at a cost of ₨ 1,25,00,000; the full project is estimated at approximately ₨ 5,00,00,000.
