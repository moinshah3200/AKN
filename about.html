About the Trust

Established on 11/09/2013, AKN Trust serves Pakistan under one motto: the best of people are those who bring benefit to people — ایک نئی امید, a new hope.

Introduction

Who we are

Anwar Khatm-e-Nabuwat Trust Pakistan — انوار ختم نبوت ٹرسٹ پاکستان — was established on 11 September 2013 under the patronage and supervision of Fazilat-ush-Sheikh Hazrat Maulana Abdul Hafeez Makki رحمۃ اللہ علیہ and Hazrat Maulana Hafiz Sagheer Ahmad رحمت اللہ.

Its purpose is the defence of the belief in Khatm-e-Nabuwat — the finality of Prophethood ﷺ — and the protection of the honour of the Prophet ﷺ; the kafalat (support) of new Muslims; and da’wah through welfare and public-benefit works, alongside educational, social and welfare services across Pakistan.

Our mission

What we defend, and why

Two callings sit at the heart of the Trust’s work — guarding a core belief of Islam, and standing beside those who embrace it.

Defence of Khatm-e-Nabuwat

The Trust exists to defend the belief in the finality of Prophethood ﷺ and to protect the honour of the Prophet ﷺ. It pursues this through da’wah on the Prophetic method with sincerity, through literature and books, courses across Pakistan’s cities, and by resolving the doubts of thousands of Muslims.

Kafalat of new Muslims

Those who accept Islam often face social boycott and isolation. The Trust answers with kafalat — ration and stipends, employment and interest-free loans, housing and rent support, education for their children, nikah arrangements for their daughters, and free residence at Darul Kafalah in Lahore.

Where we work

From the markaz to the desert

The Trust’s work radiates from its markaz in Chenab Nagar and its Lahore office, reaching across Punjab and into interior Sindh.

Markaz — Chenab Nagar

Head office in Chenab Nagar (Mohalla Nusratabad), District Chiniot, Punjab — home of the da’wah work, monthly stipends for widows and orphans, hand pumps in 20 homes, and the Rafiq Medical Center now under construction.

Lahore office

55-Ghani Street, Rajgarh, Lahore — the central office, beside the two-storey Darul Kafalah offering free residence to new Muslims, with a college planned at the Khatm-e-Nabuwat office Rajgarh.

Punjab & interior Sindh

Quranic makatib across Punjab and interior Sindh; wells and pumps in Tharparkar — Mithi, Khokhrapar, Nangarpar and Islamkot; water filtration plants in Punjab; and Khatm-e-Nabuwat courses across Pakistan’s cities.

Alhamdulillah

Report of Acceptance of Islam

Through the Trust’s da’wah on the Prophetic method, hundreds of Qadianis have accepted Islam — among them Shams-ud-Din, foster nephew of the current Qadiani head Mirza Masroor, former murabbis Amir Munir, Nazir Ahmad and Shahid Ahmad, former mufti Muhammad Ahmad, and many members of the Mirza family — while the doubts of thousands of Muslims have been resolved.

A welcome, not just a witness

Every acceptance of Islam is followed by kafalat — dawat arrangements, Taleef-e-Qalb support, education for children, employment, housing and rishtas — so that no one who embraces Islam is left to face the social boycott alone.
